Before you start

You need your TV Kings username and password from your welcome message.

About the warnings: TV Kings isn't installed from the Google Play Store, so your device will ask you to confirm before it installs. You may see "File might be harmful" or a Google Play Protect message. That's normal for any app installed this way. The steps below show exactly what to tap. You don't need to turn off Play Protect or any other security setting.

Using the old SlayerTV app? TV Kings installs next to it. Uninstall SlayerTV once you've signed in to TV Kings.

Android phone or tablet

  1. On your phone, tap Download TV Kings at the top of this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Download. If Chrome says "File might be harmful", tap Download anyway.
  3. When it finishes, tap Open. You can also find the file in your Downloads or Files app.
  4. If your phone says it isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then tap the back arrow.
  5. Tap Install.
  6. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway. The exact words vary a little between phones.
  7. Tap Open. TV Kings is now in your apps.
Samsung phones: if the install is blocked by Auto Blocker, go to Settings > Security and privacy > Auto Blocker and turn it off. Install TV Kings, then turn Auto Blocker back on.

Amazon Firestick or Fire TV

  1. On the home screen, select Find (the magnifying glass), search for Downloader and install it. It has an orange icon.
  2. Go to Settings > My Fire TV > Developer options > Install unknown apps and turn Downloader on.
    • Don't see Developer options? Go to Settings > My Fire TV > About, select your device name (for example "Fire TV Stick 4K") 7 times until it says you're a developer, then go back.
  3. Open Downloader. Allow access if it asks.
  4. In the box, type rohh1.github.io/tvkings-download and select Go.
  5. This page opens. Scroll to Download TV Kings and select it.
  6. When the download finishes, select Install.
  7. Select Done. When Downloader offers to delete the install file, select Delete. You don't need it any more.
  8. Open TV Kings from Your Apps (the three squares on the home screen). To keep it handy, highlight it, press the menu button (☰) and choose Move to front.

Android TV and Google TV boxes

  1. Open the Google Play Store on your TV, search for Downloader and install it.
  2. Let Downloader install apps:
    • Google TV (Chromecast with Google TV and most newer TVs): go to Settings > System > About and select Android TV OS build 7 times to turn on developer mode. Then go to Settings > Apps > Security & restrictions > Unknown sources and turn Downloader on.
    • Other Android TV boxes: go to Settings > Device Preferences > Security & restrictions > Unknown sources and turn Downloader on.
  3. Open Downloader, type rohh1.github.io/tvkings-download and select Go. Then select Download TV Kings on this page.
  4. Select Install. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, select More details, then Install anyway.
  5. Select Done. TV Kings now appears in your apps row.

Signing in for the first time

  1. Open TV Kings.
  2. Enter your username and password. Leave Server as it is.
  3. Select Sign in.

Your login is saved, so you only do this once. The first load can take up to a minute while your channels and movies download. After that, the app opens straight away.

Got an M3U link instead of a username and password? Select Server > Change and paste the whole link. TV Kings fills in the rest.

On a TV, use the arrow keys to move between boxes and press OK to type.

Getting around

  • Favourites: long-press any channel, or tap the star while it's playing. Favourites show first in Live TV and on Home.
  • TV Guide: see what's on for the next 24 hours. Shows with a clock icon have already aired and can be watched again. On a show that's on now, open it and choose Start over.
  • Continue watching: movies and episodes pick up where you left off.
  • Sports: shows the games on now and later today, and which channel has each one.
  • Parental controls: adult categories are hidden by default. To change that or set a PIN, go to Account > Settings.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
"App not installed"Delete any older copy of TV Kings, make sure you have at least 100 MB free, then download it again.
Install blocked by Play ProtectTap More details, then Install anyway.
"Didn't accept this username and password"Retype both carefully. They're case-sensitive, so check capital letters and remove any spaces.
"Subscription has expired"Go to Account > Renew now.
"Reached its screen limit"Your plan has a set number of screens. Close TV Kings on another device and try again.
Channel keeps freezingThe app reconnects on its own. If it keeps happening, restart your router and set Account > Settings > Buffer to Extra stable.
Channels or movies missingGo to Account > Refresh channels & library.
A channel has no guide infoSome channels don't have a guide. The channel itself still works.

Still stuck? Message the team you got your subscription from. Include your username, your device (for example "Firestick 4K" or "Samsung S24"), and a photo of any error on screen.
